# Stage 2 targeted re-screen — new continuous standards depth, 2026-10-13
Version: Stage-3 revisit of Ghosh–Morita-style real cost saving and standards-endogenous differentiation. Claims are strictly bounded to the validated model; the previous Stage2 gate and its source-coverage limitations are retained.

1. **Bourreau & Doğan (2010),** *Cooperation in Product Development and Process R&D between Competitors*, IJIO 28(2), pp 176–190, DOI 10.1016/j.ijindorg.2009.07.010. Publisher abstract/introduction specifically examine PARTIAL cooperation determining product commonality, saved development costs, increased competition and R&D. **Very close on endogenous depth and resource costs.**
2. **Ghosh & Morita (2012),** *Competitor Collaboration and Product Distinctiveness*, IJIO 30(2), pp 137–152, DOI 10.1016/j.ijindorg.2011.07.003. Publisher abstract/intro show common value-chain cost savings, reduced member differentiation, enhanced distinction from outsider, and market competitive/welfare consequences. **Very close but their leading model uses price competition and product-location moves, not this exact LQ Cournot club.**
3. **Economides & Skrzypacz (2003/04),** *Standards Coalitions Formation and Market Structure in Network Industries*, author/institution paper https://archive.nyu.edu/bitstream/2451/26158/2/3-8.pdf. Explicit endogenous network standards coalitions and downstream oligopoly reduced-form. **Very close bargaining/network industry predecessor.**
4. **Lukach, Kort & Plasmans (2007),** *Standard-Creating Coalitions in Oligopoly with Differentiated Products and Variable Network Density*, DOI 10.2139/ssrn.996856. Abstract/metadata verify differentiated-product standard coalitions and R&D; full theorem/proof not accessed, so NO unique-claim or novelty pass can be certified against it.
5. **Hart & Kurz (1983),** *Endogenous Formation of Coalitions*, Econometrica 51(4), pp 1047–1064, DOI 10.2307/1912051. Unanimity, coalitional stability and nonexistence in general; the exactly matched-message game and Pareto frontier are NOT automatically novel.
6. **Maggi & Ossa (2023),** *The Political Economy of International Regulatory Cooperation*, AER 113(8), pp 2168–2200, DOI 10.1257/aer.20200780. Government standard/regulatory cooperation with political pressure, but no separate technology-owning firm veto in the accessed model.

**Stage2 verdict for NEW model: CONDITIONALLY SCREENED, not standalone novelty-certified.** The additional terms h and f cannot be called a new mechanism just because of different functional forms. The derived interval between distinct government and firm optimal depths is a generic multi-party Pareto-agreement result. Economic novelty would require a further nontrivial network/differentiation/coalition effect not an immediate corollary of these authors or Hart–Kurz. Therefore Stage4 construction can be recorded as solved, but author should not submit a paper on this result alone.

## THEOREM ST19.1 (main, STRONGER than Stage18)

In the **three-country SIMPLE-MAJORITY + UNIVERSAL RIGHT-TO-PROPOSE** variant, NO initial private technology profile with **exactly TWO firms selecting any OPEN O(c_i,a_i) and the remaining firm N**, with arbitrary different continuous design choices 0<=a_i<=c_i<=1, can result in a PRIVATE TWO-MEMBER regional standard RS under policy L in a pure-strategy full game SPE.

In other words, the entire two-O/one-N private RS nonintervention **OUTCOME CLASS** is eliminated. This is **not** a proof that all private/non-universal standards, every non-monotone strategy profile, all three-firm O configurations, or mixed equilibria disappear.

**Exact mathematical proof and asymmetric reduction**

1. Assume an initial h=(O(c_i,a_i),O(c_j,a_j),N) is a putative majority+all-proposals pure SPE leading to final L and RS. If, e.g., a_i>a_j, firm i can REDUCE just a_i to a_j, preserving the min-matched regional common design, firm productivity and all three Cournot quantities and consumer surplus. This releases strictly positive real private capital `phi(a_i²-a_j²)/2`. Since the new public M mandates the same O(1,0), total already-installed-plus-incremental mandatory capital decreases by exactly the same amount. The national δ_i(M−L) remains UNCHANGED for all 3 governments, so the government policy L remains a credible choice under the same tie rule. Thus putative SPE cannot have unequal a_i,a_j.
2. When a_i=a_j=a but c_i>c_j, firm i can reduce c_i to c_j, maintaining effective pair match, Cournot quantities and CS, but saving real up-front capital `phi(c_i²-c_j²)/2`. At M the total public-compliant interface capital for any existing O purchaser is `phi/2(1+a²)` regardless of the initial c_i because the missing c upgrade has to be paid; it cannot undercut M by installing excess c beforehand. Consequently the owner's policy gain δ_i=M−L WEAKLY DECREASES when it reduces c_i, while the two other governments' policy differences remain unaffected. Thus a previously rejected M cannot pass merely due to this unilateral reduction; private owner earns strictly MORE with final L. So c_i=c_j is necessary in any such pure L-RS SPE. Stage5's independent exact Fraction 3×3 asymmetric Cournot implementation confirms the invariance and exact cost saving with unequal c,a.
3. Therefore any candidate L-RS is necessarily a **symmetric two-private-member** h=(O(c,a),O(c,a),N), 0<=a<=c<=1. For a∈[0,.75], the Stage18 independent exact rational THREE-dimensional tensor Bernstein polynomial certificate proves `B(c,a,eta)>0` (strict national **REGULATORY WELFARE GAIN**) for EACH of its two member governments, uniformly in the entire eta-c-a physical rectangle. Numerator 125/125 and denominator 75/75 coefficients strictly positive. Since both member governments now have public initiative rights, one proposes public M and the other approves; the simple majority passes M. Thus final policy cannot be L.
4. For a>.75, c>=a>.75, if B(c,a,eta)>=0 member government(s) have initiative and 2 YES, again policy M, contradiction. If B(c,a,eta)<0, Stage18 exact Bernstein shows `∂P_R(c,a,eta)/∂a<0` for all eta∈[.10,.14], c,a∈[.75,1], with all 224 numerator and 196 denominator coefficients positive for the NEGATIVE derivative. One founder can lower only its a slightly, changing the effective matched common design. By strict governmental B<0 and continuity, both member governments still reject M, and the outsider alone cannot pass 2 votes. The firm's payoff strictly improves. Contradiction. If B=0, the tie-to-YES proposal/vote rule makes M pass; no knife-edge L exception.

Each step uses global real choice-space arguments rather than finite design-grid scans. Thus **all asymmetric two-member OPEN RS initial profiles** are excluded under the combined constitutional reform in the reduced no-transfers game. The proof is stronger than just excluding privately optimized h=(O(1,a_R^*),O(1,a_R^*),N).

## Delimited intellectual contribution and important limitations

The observation that majority reforms and right-to-propose reforms interact through endogenous standards coalitions is a sharper and falsifiable result than merely stating 'a majority can defeat a third-country veto'. Yet **political agenda gatekeeping and international veto rights are already studied**; Takarada et al. (2020) already analyze nonmember country blocking of global harmonization, while Ghosh–Morita (2012), Gil Moltó et al. (2005) and Lukach–Kort–Plasmans (2007) pose serious prior-art threats for technology design, differentiated standardization coalitions and regional stability. Actual theoretical novelty must be demonstrated as **a nonredundant joint comparative institutional prediction on initial firms' continuous investments**, not assumed from familiar ingredients.

**Scope critically different from Stage17/18:** there are NO treasury side payments in this constitutional 2×2 design. If conditional government donations are reintroduced, vote/payoff indifference and donor Nash selection can re-open delicate knife-edge support; Stage19 has deliberately not proven full treaty-augmented global asymmetric RS exclusion. It excludes exactly TWO private OPEN adopters and ONE legacy N with final NONINTERVENTION L, not every possible partially interoperable standards market structure or all equilibria (such as three O with differing designs). It also assumes universal public initiative proposals may be submitted by ANY of the three sovereign countries but the same majority rule still governs adoption, regardless of who sponsors.

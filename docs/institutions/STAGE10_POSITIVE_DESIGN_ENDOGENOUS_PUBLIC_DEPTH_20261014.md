## 6. Limits, literature and next research gates

- Original six physical/network product primitives are held FIXED for both constructed Stage10 equilibria, but changed ETA from Stage9; the Stage9 welfare difference and Stage10 reversal are not a comparative-static proof of uniqueness of a threshold eta; a full eta-K bifurcation diagram is pending.
- The public O(cP,aP) family is exogenously **available** at all feasible depths; government choice only picks among pre-existing legally usable designs. A government R&D cost/technology possibility set or public procurement rights would need separate primitives.
- Government agenda G1 and the order of public vote vs G1's depth specification choice are institutional rules. National government vetoes and policy approvals are endogenous GIVEN those rules; the rules themselves aren't constitutionally optimized.
- Private matching uses minimum of independently developed design stocks, and member-outsider substitutes are the particular Cournot proxy. User's desired Ghosh–Morita distinctiveness channel is NOW economically active in the interior private SPE, but the reduced-form demand is not a unique engineering microfoundation.
- No proof of full seven-dimensional theta-K closed-form phase partition, no unique standard-type equilibrium, not all possible mixed starting profiles analyzed. Two existence regions on a rational theta witness, now with endogenously positive private design and endogenously chosen government depths, are rigorously established.
- Prior art: Ghosh & Morita (2012), *Competitor collaboration and product distinctiveness*, IJIO 30(2) 137–152, DOI 10.1016/j.ijindorg.2011.07.003, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167718711000671.

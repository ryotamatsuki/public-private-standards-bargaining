## ST20.2 — Any singleton OPEN developer (1-O): ALL three governments STRICTLY want M, for ALL real designs

With a single O(c,a) inventor and two N firms, there is NO matched two-member private O standard; the Stage5 exact market technology definition gives **baseline N Cournot production**, only the sole developer pays real K and variable physical capital. Define `W_N` as per-country N market welfare, `W_I^0` as national full public IS welfare net of phi/2 implementation but before new family setup K.

Two N-country governments strictly gain
`Delta_N=W_I^0-K-W_N>0`
even at maximum K=.56.

The sole developer country switches to the already-open public O(1,0), pays **only the positive missing compatibility c-capital** under within-O irreversible retrofit, and cannot recoup its wasted initial a stock. Its per-national gain is exactly
`Delta_O=W_I^0-W_N+phi*c²/2>0`
for EVERY real `0<=a<=c<=1`. The upfront original developer K and its sunk a design appear in BOTH alternatives and cancel. These positive inequalities are analytical GLOBAL, not sampled. Thus unanimity and majority BOTH enact public O(1,0) after any singleton private tech, regardless of which national firm invested.

Note this is a terminal public-policy theorem at singleton histories. It does not claim a singleton O initial history is an SPE; any redundant founder investment might be profitably avoided.

Suppose all firms independently choose the SAME `O(c,a)` with any feasible real design. There are three matched compatibility pairs. Let
`b(c,a)=gamma0+kappa*a-vc`, `z(c)=1+rho*c`, `m=1-v`,

`G(c,a)=3m q_I²+3(m+2b)q_I²/2` be national gross public/product-market welfare BEFORE the real physical technology fees.

The private national status quo L welfare is `G(c,a)-K-phi(c²+a²)/2`.
Under mandated existing public O(1,0), the initial O family is reused, c retrofits to 1, the old positive a-capital is sunk and cannot be reimbursed: public national W is `G(1,0)-K-phi*(1+a²)/2`. Thus exactly

`Delta_i(c,a)=G(1,0)-G(c,a)-phi*(1-c²)/2`
` = [W_I(1,0)-W_I(c,0)] +[G(c,0)-G(c,a)]>=0`,

where `W_I(c,0)=G(c,0)-phi c²/2` is national welfare with freshly financed homogeneous private design.

The first bracket is nonnegative and strictly positive when c<1 because **Stage11 proves `∂W_I(c,0)/∂c>0` over the ENTIRE closed [0,1]** by rational Sturm; the second bracket nonnegative and strictly positive when a>0 because

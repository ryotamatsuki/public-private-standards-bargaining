# Stage 4 — Institutional Robustness: Proprietary Licensing, Three-Firm Independent Adoption, and Government Authority
2026-10-14 JST. Parent Draft PR #32. User authorized continuation toward independent private competing standards, licensing rights and governmental institution choice. **Results are a rigorous adversarial diagnostic, not a completed unrestricted multi-platform/3-government constitutional game.**

## 1. Scientific conclusion (read BEFORE interpreting Stage3)

Two crucial policy results of Stage3 are **institution-dependent**, not unconditional economic laws.

1. **Open-IP restriction drives government-induced IS:** if a private developer can license an essential interface to F2 **but lawfully EXCLUDE F3 and regulators**, a private bilateral RS consortium can preempt an internationally welfare-superior compulsory standard. This is an endogenous discrete licensing action coupled to the same globally optimized continuous compatibility and design `(c,a)`. Previous open-technology Stage3 eta=.03 had ALL SPE type IS; under endogenous IP licensing here, ALL final SPE outcomes have **closed bilateral RS**, despite the original state's preference for IS.
2. **Appointed proposer assumption drives the Stage3 fragmentation impossibility:** if firms choose incompatible interface labels INDEPENDENTLY and their first investment lacks benefits without a matching adopter, fragmented `NS` survives a strict unilateral-deviation Nash screen EVEN at zero additional fixed setup K. Independent platform coordination changes which equilibrium correspondence is relevant. Do not generalize Stage3's K=0 => nonfragmentation beyond its binding project proposal/acceptance protocol.

Both use the original six economically quantitative primitives and the Stage2 separable `C(c,a)=phi(c²+a²)/2` cost, with NO additional K or royalty coefficient. IP licence choice and asynchronous vs simultaneous adoption are new **institutional actions**.

## 2. Module A: EXACT sequential bilateral/IP-licensing backward induction, continuous design

### Actors and legal feasibility
- F1, as the **assigned private essential-interface developer**, chooses no development, a bilateral standard with a lawful **EXCLUSIVE** license for F2 only, a bilateral **OPEN** interface available to F2 and F3, or a direct OPEN 3-firm international consortium. Firm proposer also chooses **both continuous** `0<=a<=c<=1` (develop if c>0), at real adopter cost `C(c,a)=phi(c²+a²)/2`.
- F2 accepts/rejects a bilateral contract and F2/F3 accept the proposed IS contract; all absent signers retain NS.
- For an OPEN regional consortium, the third firm has a LEGAL OPTION to independently adopt the same open interface. This is endogenous third-party private adoption, unlike the previous Stage3 convention that its only possible adoption happens upon government mandate. If F3 voluntarily adopts, three-firm IS Cournot follows; if it does not, G1/G2/G3 can sequentially unanimously mandate international compatibility under the legally OPEN interface, followed by F3 compliance-vs-exit decision and Cournot.
- For a proprietary bilateral interface F1/F2 have enforceable freedom to refuse outsider essential patent access; regulators **cannot compel license availability** or freely repurpose proprietary interface technology. The regulator's public alternative-development instrument is NOT in this stage's menu. Government action is still endogenous when an open interface is available but its enforcement agenda is not freely chosen.
- F3 adopts OPEN RS technology voluntarily if `P_I(c,a)>P_O(c,a)` where P_O is its regional outsider operating payoff; an incumbent developer cannot prevent such accession if legally OPEN. Under a mandated interface, exiting gives zero and conforming yields `P_I(c,a)`, so F3 complies whenever `P_I>=0`. This is a difference in outside options, NOT a second payoff technology.
- Post-adoption product markets are ALWAYS the exact Cournot/KKT NS, RS or IS quantities and home-surplus accounting of Stage2 and Stage3. No royalties or double-counted developer costs.
- Invitees accept if realized continuation P exceeds `P_N`. The designated proposed bilateral pair is firm1+firm2; uniform relabeling gives the other pairs. Nonparticipating F3 remains an economically active Cournot outsider in these witnesses (not zero product-market output).

### General exhaustive result — contingent on legal exclusion and symmetric proposal rights
Let `U_R=max_{0<=a<=c<=1} P_R(c,a)`, `U_I=max_{0<=a<=c<=1} P_I(c,a)`, and `P_N` as before; assume these maxima are attained, invited firms can be given free licence rights and pay their actual implementation costs, and government regulation cannot force licensing of excludable IP.

**THEOREM 4A:** If `U_R>max{U_I,P_N}`, the developer's SPE final standard outcome is a PROPRIETARY bilateral RS at a private global maximizer of P_R, provided at such an optimizer an OPEN license would either admit F3 or trigger an enforceable publicly preferred IS change. More generally, `U_R` is ALWAYS an attainable private payoff through the exclusive RS choice, without public veto. Every other open-RS offer produces a payoff `<=max{U_R,U_I}`, and every direct IS offer produces `<=U_I`; hence any final SPE payoff is `max{P_N,U_R,U_I}`. If `U_I>max{U_R,P_N}` international direct design is selected. Ties can generate multiple standard types, and open RS can survive alongside exclusive RS if it matches the private max without inducing outsider adoption or compulsory IS. This is a **specific licensing protocol theorem**, not a general conclusion that government policy never affects standards: independent public open technology, competition-law mandated licensing, licensing payments and enforcement expenses are excluded.

**Certified welfare counterexample:** Fix `(v,gamma0,kappa,rho,phi)=(.01,.14,.02,.32,.14)`, `eta=.03`, K=0. Exactly over ALL continuous `(c,a)` triangle:
- strict global RS private optimum `(c_R,a_R)=(1,0)`, `U_R=8486784637/8564004980≈0.9909831506` (RS profit strictly increasing c and decreasing a, proven via exact rational inequality and Sturm root exclusion);
- strict global IS private optimum `(c_I,a_I)=(1,0)`, `U_I=301481/313600≈0.9613552296`;
- `P_N≈0.5814864124`.
So `U_R>U_I>P_N` STRICT.
- At EXACT would-be open RS developer optimum (1,0), F3 prefers joining: `P_I(1,0)≈.961355>P_O(1,0)≈.525228`; hence OPEN R cannot retain private RS payoffs.
- Incumbent government also strictly prefers a three-country IS mandate at that point: `W_I(1,0)≈1.612463>W_R(1,0)≈1.548999`; the outsider government `W_{O,R}(1,0)≈1.083244<W_I`.
- All-country aggregate national welfare would rise by `3W_I-2W_R-W_{O,R}≈0.656147>0` if all parties could apply the private open design. Yet F1 optimally grants an exclusive bilateral right; government lacks legal means to force the relevant license; final SPE is **proprietary RS**, rather than Stage3 OPEN-rights prediction IS.
- For `eta=.14`, global bilateral optimum remains `c_R=1,a_R≈.59495148`, `U_R≈1.00862094>U_I≈.961355`. Outsider would JOIN an open (1,a_R) pair because `P_I(1,a_R)≈.915007>P_O(1,a_R)≈.656457`; exclusion also supports proprietary RS. Global c monotonic and unique a peak again certified with Sturm roots.

**Interpretation:** ownership of a critical implementation right changes the equilibrium-policy outcome even with IDENTICAL underlying network, product design and government welfare preferences. The reduction is not 'government fails to vote' but **the privately developed technology is not in the government's legally feasible set**. That is the economically relevant missing institutional mechanism in Stage3.

## 3. Module B: THREE firms independently choose among incompatible interface labels

This is intentionally a DIFFERENT CONTROLLED INSTITUTION from Module A; it is **NOT** its continuation game. Three F_i simultaneously choose `N` (legacy/no investment), `X` or `Y` (technologically incompatible vendor/interface protocol labels, same ex ante design parameters). There is NO privileged common proposer, no enforceable coalition matching announcement, no ex-post renegotiation, no automatic outsider onboarding, and no public imposition of a technology that zero firms have developed. For this first exact finite normal-form test use `eta=.03,K=0,c=1,a=0` (the design itself remains controlled/fixed in this particular module). **Full independent design optimization conditional on rival labels is future work.**

Physical payoffs:
- 3 identical X or 3 identical Y: all 3 producers implement one fully compatible protocol, IS participant profit `P_I`.
- 2 matching adopters in X or Y and one unmatched: matched pair RS participant profit `P_R`; outsider chooses N and obtains `P_O` or instead chooses another singleton label, making `P_O-C(c,a)` due unsuccessful sunk deployment cost.
- 0/1 matching adopters: all products compete in original NS market; individual adopting solo pays its actual sunk `C(c,a)>0` but gets no compatibility/consortium efficiency advantage. N chooser pays nothing.
This is a deliberately specified **minimum scale for technology benefits**, consistent with bilateral or greater network consortium structure; it would change if a stand-alone developer could receive productivity gain rho without joining another firm.

All 27 action profiles (3^3) and all 3 firm unilateral deviations among N/X/Y are **EXACTLY enumerated** and evaluated with symbolic rational Cournot payoffs. ALL and ONLY the pure Nash standard choices are:
`(N,N,N)`, `(X,X,X)`, `(Y,Y,Y)`.

The no-standard (NNN) outcome is **STRICT Nash** even when K=0, because a unilateral protocol investment c>0 without a second adopter imposes positive `phi(c²+a²)/2` real cost but yields no shared network/technical benefit. This logic holds for any continuous 0<c<=1, 0<=a<=c at **positive phi**, not just the finite fixed-design case, subject to the stated singleton technology assumption. Conversely all-X and all-Y are Nash because deviating alone leaves the other two as RS and gives the deviator lower outsider profit than its IS participant profit. Two adopters plus an outsider is NOT Nash in the OPEN-label stage because the outsider would join the compatible pair, obtaining `P_I>P_O`.

**CRITICAL:** The Stage3 K=0 no-fragmentation proof was for the centralized voluntary complete-IS offer: one proposal and three individually approving signers can coordinate a joint deviation from the legacy standard. The new simultaneous independent-choice game has only **UNILATERAL** Nash deviations, hence fails coordination even though all producers would jointly benefit. It is not a mathematical contradiction. Strong Nash of this matching game would block NNN through the three-party joint change, but that is a separate refinement and would not be an SPE of the sequential IP game.

## 4. Combined interpretation, limits and targeted next work

This stage does NOT claim fully endogenous incompatible platform adoption and governmental policy simultaneously in ONE unrestricted extensive game. It supplies two sharp institutional-robustness results:
(A) sequential private license/continuous design choice can undo all-country standardization EVEN when it raises global national welfare;
(B) independent platform adoption can sustain persistent fragmentation WITHOUT fixed setup K through coordination failure alone.
The next objective is one integrated institutional protocol with independent platform choices PLUS ex ante government public open-alternative development/recognition, legally feasible rights, and endogenous continuous c,a for each firm (not just a shared proposal), including nontrivial private-versus-public technology quality and adoption thresholds. A public regulator cannot create unlicensed private technologies, but it may be able to sponsor an open alternative: that will require specifying public real development costs or a real technology productivity advantage. Such instrument effects cannot be assumed to follow automatically from a new voting label.

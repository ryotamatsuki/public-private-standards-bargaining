## Most serious remaining referee attacks (explicit red-team gate)

**R1. Technological public fallback is exogenously perfect and legally available.** Universal O(1,0) has identical publicly fixed reference; a firm adopting it never pays a second K. The apparent dominance of M in most histories could be a direct artifact of this strong institutional capability. **Mitigation**: frame public availability as a substantive identifying condition and isolate it in an Assumption or Example, not a general theorem of standardization.

**R2. 3-country combinatorics risks tautology.** Two proposers intersect any size-2 favorable government coalition, and with deterministic YES-on-ties cannot be prevented from enacting M; this is an elementary set-transversal lemma. **Mitigation**: treat coalition intersection as necessary *institutional* observation; the real nontrivial step is PROVING at least two favorable governments at every investable candidate that could survive ex ante firm best responses, despite heterogeneous, real triangular O stocks.

**R3. Pure-strategy equilibrium only.** Mixed equilibria and alternate government tie selections are not characterized. **Mitigation**: state “pure-strategy SPE final outcome” literally everywhere; no claims about unique equilibrium in general.

**R4. Significant new prior-art collision.** Ruiz (2004) already does endogenous location choice under government compatibility/recognition decisions; Costinot (2008) already obtains policy-regime ranking reversals; Farrell–Simcoe (2012) already links consensus rule, neutral intervention, and ex ante innovation. **Mitigation**: resist falsely broad “first to combine product differentiation and policy” novelty; require precise game-level comparisons.

**R5. Parameter calibration.** The 7D box supports a local existence result but remains narrow in v,gamma. No necessary/sufficient full economic parameter-space cutoffs have been proved. **Mitigation**: describe *institutional-cardinality iff conditional on physical-parameter rectangle*, NOT general economic iff or a sharp physical threshold.

**R6. Sunk capital semantics.** Ex-ante versus ex-post welfare comparisons have distinct baselines. **Mitigation**: show exact W_IS^ante−W_RS^ante=K_W-K and W_M^retrofit−W_RS^observed=K_E-K, plus K_W-K_E=phi a_R*², on one line in the paper.

**R7. Proof reproducibility versus independence.** The Stage11–27 CI is syntactic/exact algebraic verification of sufficient polynomial signs and game lemmas. Independent expert review of the full sequencing of histories and off-path votes is still needed. **Mitigation**: freeze technical assumptions; Stage30 referee audit must reconstruct equilibrium tree without trusting statements in code comments.

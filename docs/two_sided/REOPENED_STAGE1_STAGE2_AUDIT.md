## Scope and novelty baseline
Ghosh & Morita (2012) DOI 10.1016/j.ijindorg.2011.07.003 https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167718711000671. Verified publisher ABSTRACT with 3 firms on Salop circle, two collaborators move closer and away from outsider; internal differentiation decreases, external differentiation increases, collaborator prices fall and outsider price rises in their model; outsider profit can either rise or fall. This proves **two-direction distinctiveness** is OLD. Our proposed η=κ/2 respects geometric RELATIVE ARC SHIFTS, but no author claim that Cournot linear demand γ maps exactly to Salop distance.
Bourreau & Doğan (2010) DOI 10.1016/j.ijindorg.2009.07.010 https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167718709000794. Joint product components, cost sharing, partial cooperative depth, process R&D and pricing/competition already endogenized. Cost-saving h/f as source model is not itself new.
Economides & Skrzypacz (2003) SSRN 378340 https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=378340. Firm-standard coalitions with network effects, competitive feedback and oligopoly reduced-form; direct whole-game threat.
Lukach, Kort & Plasmans (2007) DOI 10.2139/ssrn.996856 https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=996856. Differentiated oligopoly/network density/standards coalitions+R&D: full theorem access NOT achieved (binding open).
Hart & Kurz (1983) DOI 10.2307/1912051: one-shot coalition coordination and coalition stability are inherited general results, NOT new.

- Lukach–Kort–Plasmans (2007) https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/standard-creating-coalitions-in-oligopoly-with-differentiated-pro/ : differentiated standards coalitions plus network intensity and R&D quality, theorem access limited.
- Ghosh–Morita (2012) DOI 10.1016/j.ijindorg.2011.07.003 : common development SAVES REAL COST and changes inside/outside differentiation, under PRICE competition in their leading model; our current κ(s) is an assumption, not the authors' fully microfounded product-location equilibrium.
- Baake–Boom (2001), DOI 10.1016/S0167-7187(99)00029-6, publisher INTRO: quality + installed-base network and mutually agreed compatibility; any claim 'network + endogenous private choices' new is killed.

- Japan METI SEP licensing guidance https://www.meti.go.jp/policy/economy/chizai/sep_license/index.html and JFTC patent pool standards guideline https://www.jftc.go.jp/dk/guideline/unyoukijun/patent.html: REAL IP licensing rights/competition policy; does **not** imply every technical standard legally needs a separate sovereign approval or that a patent holder can withhold licences without antitrust consequences. ## 5. Short editorial contribution memo / strongest hostile objection
*Provisional candidate*: Market-specific consumer welfare and private per-unit royalty rents can disagree on whether a technically interoperable, more homogenous platform ought to receive public recognition. The two-key recognition/licensing structure may then constrain which international standards survive coordinated proposals. Counterfactual learning: how licensing incidence, compatibility, and product distinctiveness jointly determine an IMPLEMENTABLE rather than merely privately profitable standard.

*Hostile objection*: \`W=Π+CS\` and \`W<0<Π\` is a textbook participation veto, and the example's shallow interface Pareto-dominates its deep platform. Nothing was yet shown about a NONTRIVIAL surviving coalition or institution selection, nor proved that Γ coalition games + general royalty Cournot cannot directly imply the payoff inequalities. Therefore pure component novelty is KILLED; full-game mechanism hypothesis remains **UNCERTIFIED**. If Stage 4 cannot identify a real coalition or contract/welfare selection reversal on a proper parameter region without changing feasible sets, stop the current source model or seek explicit author-approved rollback to Stage1 to introduce a defensible *real* common-platform efficiency mechanism.

## 7. Stage-4 top-level PRE-REGISTERED test
Determine whether, over an OPEN parameter region and fixed final contract choice sets,
 (a) there is a contract z with firm assent but government veto,
 (b) alternative permissible contracts fail to dominate away this veto,
 (c) government approval changes what standard/coalition is supported by a defensible coalition stability/refinement (not just the known all-SQ Nash),
 (d) at least one of κ, v, r is structurally necessary in a specified *THEOREM* (not the isolated witness),
 (e) the result cannot be obtained as a direct Hart–Kurz/Yi/Gallini/standard Cournot corollary.
Any failure of (b)–(e) means downgrade the current project; NO new features to rescue signs without Stage1/2 rollback. A real cost-savings parameter is only a possible separate source-model revision, **not adopted**.

## Important explanatory distinctions
This gate does NOT conclude "one prior paper contains the exact 6-player government/firm standards negotiation game." None inspected fully was shown to do so. Instead, it concludes **none of the actually proven claims changes equilibrium standard identity in a way that requires both types of decision-maker**, while every other observed comparative fact is contained in or a standard corollary of prior economic principles. That is enough to FAIL the Stage6 novelty burden for the CURRENT set of results, while the general architecture might contain yet-unexplored theorems.

## Predecessor reversal boundary (needed to avoid invented conflict)
EF98 (Economides–Flyer 1998) finds full compatibility with relatively weak network effects and often fractured standards with strong network dependence; ES03 (2003) finds full when effects very strong. The equilibrium concept, product-valuation law, timing and contract formation are materially different. Candidate's low-v RS/high-v IS is a **CHANGED COMPARISON OBJECT**, not same-domain contradiction to EF98 and not new result relative to ES03. No novel paradox claimed.

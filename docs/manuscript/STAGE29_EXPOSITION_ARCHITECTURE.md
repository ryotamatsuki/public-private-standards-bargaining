## One-sentence question and contribution

Question: Can a national coalition favor a common public compatibility mandate yet fail to obtain it because its supporting governments lack the legal agenda right, and can the change in agenda rights alter the **entire set of privately sustainable terminal pure-SPE standard outcomes**, not merely an ex-post vote?

Contribution: In a specified three-country open-technology sunk-investment game, two independent public proposers are **necessary and sufficient** for pure terminal public-IS outcome uniqueness when at most two YES votes are required; public terminal equilibrium uniqueness need not improve world welfare; ex-ante versus ex-post welfare rankings differ by irreversible private design stock.

Not claimed: general equilibrium with transferable utility, mixed SPE, n-country universality, "first to allow endogenous product attributes under standards", "first to use two-vote standard setting", robustness necessity in 7D, welfare-optimality of majority.

## Editorial/plain-English rules

- Prefer agent/action/causal-chain language: "Firm 1 can lower the redundant stock without affecting the other market quantities or government votes," not "Clearly by standard algebra".
- When stating a theorem, provide exact domain and what is **not** uniquely identified.
- Separate ex-post observed RS history from an actual initial pure SPE under that constitution.
- One paragraph, one dominant mechanism; connect it to the next paragraph. Avoid repeated hyperbolic claims of novelty.
- Avoid technical notation proliferation. **Demand matrix H** is distinct from public government mandate M, and **government agenda set** is \\mathcal A (not the matrix).
- Define W_I NET of stock phi/2 but BEFORE fixed K and preserve the same convention in sections and appendices. Do not silently reinterpret national gross welfare GI.
- Main text retains the *proof-critical bridges*: initial O vs N public fixed costs; Cournot FOC→profits/CS; national policy gain→proposal/vote condition; maximal private investment redundancy→two favorable governments; K_W-K_E=phi a².
- All full rational polynomial manipulations and root isolations go to Appendix/verification scripts. Code pass is not proof of model or novelty.
- At every editorial revision do integrated clean LaTeX compile, undefined citation/ref audit, label collision audit, then Stage30 external hostile mathematical and literature audit.

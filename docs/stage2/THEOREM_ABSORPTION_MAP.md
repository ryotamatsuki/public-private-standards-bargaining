## 1. Rewrite the exact Stage-1 game without standards labels
Let H be a finite actor set; Z finite enforceable contract menu, P(z)⊆H required signers. Each h chooses a_h∈Z_h∪{SQ}. Contract z becomes binding iff a_h=z for every h∈P(z), with no signer available to two active contracts (exclusive messages). Payoffs u_h(a)=U_h(C(a)), where C(a) is the compatible activated set and U_h comes from an arbitrary simultaneous-market equilibrium payoff mapping. **As a matter of formal game isomorphism**, forgetting labels G/F and plugging U_h as a table gives a standard finite normal-form unanimity/matching game.

Consequences immediate from generic equilibrium theory: finite mixed NE exists; every-SQ pure NE exists when all nontrivial contracts require ≥2 signers and unilateral change cannot activate anything. A fully matched single deal is a pure NE when its necessary signatories weakly prefer it to null and no lone deviation can instantiate another contract. Strong Nash can fail by known coalition theory. **These are not new results**.

Governments own recognition authority and have W_i=CS_i + Pi_i + public net transfer; firms own essential private technical licenses and have Pi_i; each destination's differentiated-Cournot is microfounded by U=a1'q-q'M(z)q/2, Mii=1-v, Mij=gamma0+kappa*s_ij-v I_ij. An ordinary coalition structure partition of *firms only* does not automatically capture who may exercise publicly enforceable recognition across different territories. No blanket claim 'first to model both' allowed.

## Evidence warning
Cross-paper novelty is not proven by empty keyword search. Names of works, DOIs, author limitations and theorem claims are verified only to stated depth. The 2003 paper's *full* content documents that both network effects AND less differentiation inside the same technical standard, together with **standard coalitions bargaining**, were already combined, making prior broad claims about that combination untenable.

## Stage-2 canonical comparison condition
Any claim of a new public/private effect must identify (i) EXACT public legal approval and private essential-IP rights, (ii) specified veto signatories and non-signers, (iii) same admissible contracts under counterfactual, (iv) endogenous standards/coalitions, and (v) a published-theorem-irreducible prediction with a nonknifeedge parameter region.

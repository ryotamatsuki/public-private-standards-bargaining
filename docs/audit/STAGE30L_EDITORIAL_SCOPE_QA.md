> You have replaced the familiar observation "standards can be blocked" with an attractive puzzle: the politically protective government is the **noninvesting** country. Comparing **all pure equilibria** is meaningful as a *guarantee under unspecified selection*, not an expected effect of a reform, and it identifies a genuine risk of losing the best private adaptation. The reader should not be asked to accept that the theorem survives other demand/voting primitives: current research only proves it in a specified category-status and sincere decision-rule benchmark.

**Remaining editorial objections:** The exact class discontinuity at third O(0,0) and g=0 government administrative cost remain unusually convenient; Stage30 g=.04 and unrestricted all-NO tests show the central result cannot be transferred automatically. A stronger journal likely requires economic portability or a tighter institutional microfoundation.

## ECONOMIC QUESTION A — strongest EXISTING-game intellectual content: outsider-funded political insurance

**Precise question:** Can a government with *no* privately invested domestic standard be the ONLY effective political guardian of the differentiated standard developed by other countries' private firms, even when BOTH founders' own national governments want compulsory public harmonization? Can a **rise in fixed technology membership cost K** expand which private-investment stock profiles are sustainable, even though higher K conventionally deters direct investment?

**Source answers:** Stage30C C1,C3,C4,C7 + Stage30N: yes on certified parameter rectangle and explicitly restricted A/r policy selector, including Stage30P simultaneous stagewise admissibility or a DIFFERENT sequential political protocol. For two founders' O(c,a), N outsider, govt approval signs (+,+,-), supported iff private feasibility F_a>=0, F>=P and outsider K>V in private-preserving constitutions. Expanding agenda in same r2 A={3} -> {1,3} destroys private-terminal pure SPE; increasing K weakly expands feasible private design CORRESPONDENCE. Doesn't imply observed technology stock grows or welfare rises.

**Independent economics, if successfully defended:** investment by **two countries' businesses** shifts the regulatory costs of a **third noninvestor country**; political protection comes from a different national coalition than the investing firms' direct interests. Bilateral standards can require an outsider's prospective fresh conversion setup K to be politically safe. Distinct from Gratton/Lee's firm lobbying-free capture *of its own consumer electorate* and Takarada's no separate first-stage investing firms.

**Novelty threat:** Gandal/Shy and Takarada already have outsider nonharmonization; Gratton/Lee already firms shaping politics; C1's strong full-pure-SPE relies on categorical N/O(0,0) jump. Must show C4 is not just generic cost incidence and demonstrate genuine new testable incidence signs after alternative sensible demand branches. Verdict: MEDIUM potential as one packaged current-paper mechanism, high mathematical maturity, scientific originality CONDITIONAL. NOT a newly discovered third independent pillar.

## ECONOMIC QUESTION B — strongest UNDEREXPLOITED EXISTING PAYOFF INSIGHT: the ex-ante/ex-post implementation gap

**Question:** Can harmonization be better than privately created regional standards **if political rights are fixed before investment**, yet be impossible to implement ex post using ANY individually rational, budget-balanced compensation among all three governments at the already-installed private standard?

**Stage26/16 already imply answer YES conditional on exact source private standard h=(O(1,a*),O(1,a*),N)**.

Define:
  K_V: outsider national YES indifference,
  B>0: each founder govt public-minus-private at the installed history,
  L=K-K_V>0: outsider govt loss at h,
  K_E=K_V+2B: gross net ex-post total-welfare efficiency cutoff,
  K_W=K_E+phi*a*(eta)^2: ex-ante comparison of original private equilibrium versus separately financed public initial NNN/M path.

**Exact sign identities (for source equilibrium design h):**
  ex ante world public-M versus private-L = K_W-K,
  ex post world forced-M *after private a capital is sunk* versus preserving L = K_E-K.
  The strict wedge is K_W-K_E = phi*a*^2 >0.

When K_E<K<K_W (and baseline private equilibrium/outsider veto restrictions hold), world welfare favors a public-only ex ante constitution BUT upgrading the already installed private standard lowers the total available ex-post real surplus. Thus no balanced transfer can make EVERY government weakly better off after sunk private investment: aggregate willingness-to-pay is negative. **Ex-post transfers are not a substitute for ex ante constitutional commitment.** This is an EXACT economic distinction, not a need for more algebra, but it is not generically new relative to Choi/Jensen-Thursby/Celik/Harstad standard sunk-cost timing.

Other region: K_V<K<K_E => outsider veto even when ex post M raises total surplus; founders individually B<L cannot finance veto alone, but 2B>L makes combined donor financing feasible. Zero-pledge veto Nash can coexist with financed M Nash: generic threshold public-good coordination. K>K_W => privately supported L can be better even ex ante. Stage26 gives unique eta thresholds, but don't call inequality identities a newly discovered universal theorem.

**Novelty route:** A nontrivial **joint institutional-incompleteness trilemma** linking (i) third-country adaptation incidence, (ii) two potential national donor interests, (iii) firm sunk differentiated design, with constitutional proposal rights. Requires identifying which exact feature not derivable from cited 1996/2007/2020 papers. Research worth an economic-paper *lead question*, but current source result by itself is a compelling COROLLARY, not a confirmed new independent field theorem.

**CRITICAL NEGATIVE FILTER on prior strongest second-paper idea:** Stage16–17 show funded M at already-installed high-a OON is NOT a full firm-stage SPE because founders avoid redundant sunk a on anticipation. But in the very region where funded M is EX-POST Pareto-financeable (2B>L i.e. K<K_E), it is also true that K<K_W and the pre-investment public-only path beats that chosen old private design in AGGREGATE welfare. Thus **"transfer-induced investment collapse reduces total ex-ante welfare" does NOT follow**; do not market as a paradox or direct welfare loss. A second paper requires a genuinely new behaviorally credible full equilibrium result beyond merely rederiving NNN/M.

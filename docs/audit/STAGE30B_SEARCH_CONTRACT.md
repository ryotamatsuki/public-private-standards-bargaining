## Three questions, specified before this search's calculations

1. **Equilibrium coordination:** Is the privately optimal bilateral design only
   one member of a larger set of private SPE? Derive an explicit supported set
   or characterize the bilateral branch, including all finite unilateral
   deviations, N exits, entry and policy switches. Opposite possibility: policy
   or entry collapses the set to the already studied optimum.
2. **Public welfare invariance:** Do all public-terminal pure SPE have zero
   wasted commonality investment, making their welfare identical despite
   initial-investment multiplicity? Opposite possibility: a positive-waste
   public SPE defeats this claim. Do not infer invariance from terminal labels.
3. **Selection and comparative statics:** Does the wider private equilibrium
   set change the constitutional welfare conclusion, or yield a nontrivial
   fixed-cost comparative static? Rank complete-equilibrium claims separately
   from supported-family claims. Opposite possibility: only familiar
   min-complementarity/accounting conclusions survive.

## Finite stop

One construction/attack cycle on these three questions, followed by a targeted
parent-theorem search. Retain failed candidates. Additional propositions must
be necessary consequences of these questions, not new primitives. Deliver
proofs where feasible, label numerical evidence and unclosed classifications,
and decide whether the resulting economic insight changes the full-paper
assessment. More formulas alone are not a positive editorial decision.

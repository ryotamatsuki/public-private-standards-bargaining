## 1. Canonical candidate and two irreducible-looking claim bundles

Remove standards language and strip the government selector to its algebraic skeleton.

Three symmetric producers first choose \`N\` or private two-coordinate investments on a triangle; effective pair output benefits use coordinatewise **minimum**; individual costs include an unavoidable open-technology setup \`K\`. A public fallback \`M\` exists legally after the investments, with a stipulated **sincere voting/proposal selector** based on each government's profit-plus-consumer welfare gains. An \`N\` player alone pays new setup \`K\` after \`M\`, while an existing developer has already paid it; retooling to the common public interface destroys privately financed differentiation capital. No private transfer or licensing is allowed.

The terminal private pure-SPE set can be expressed as a politically filtered weakest-link provision correspondence:
\[
R(\eta,K)=\{(c,a):0\le a\le c\le1,\ F_a(c,a)\ge0,\ F(c,a)\ge P,\ K>V(c,a)\}.
\]
It is supportable under unanimity or a sole **outsider** agenda holder, and under no other constitution in the baseline. Every surviving private equilibrium has **two** firms with identical stocks and a noninvesting outsider (C1). In every such history the firms weakly prefer to retain private production, their home governments strictly want public adoption, while the noninvestor government strictly opposes it (C7). When at most two YES votes suffice and at least two governments can propose, all private equilibria disappear and the exact set of public-equilibrium initial profiles contains \`N\` or \`O(c,0)\` for each firm (C3). This is **Bundle A (political fallback and investment stability)**; it has a coherent economic causal chain beyond the numerical agenda cardinality.

Every public pure SPE has the *same* aggregate world welfare \`3(W_I-K)\` because all potentially wasted initial commonality stocks are zero (C2). Among all privately sustainable designs, the maximal world welfare equals \`T(1,a_R^*)-2K\` (C5). Hence a reform to a public-only constitution weakly improves world welfare against **every old pure SPE** iff \`K\le K_W(\eta)=3W_I-T(1,a_R^*)\`; if higher, the same reform strictly raises the worst attainable welfare while lowering the maximum attainable welfare (C6). This is **Bundle B (complete-equilibrium welfare guarantee)**. The last inequality itself is the generic comparison of a constant to a supremum and **not** an independently new welfare theorem.

The only defensible candidate paper joins Bundle A and Bundle B. C2, C3, C4 and C7 are important bridges/corollaries but should not be marketed as four fresh general mechanisms.

## 2. Explicit parent-to-child absorption maps

Notation for judgments:
- DIRECTLY ABSORBED: theorem or universally valid argument already provides this assertion upon a short valid restriction.
- PARTIALLY ABSORBED: key economic component or sublemma present, but the **headline specific full game** does not follow without new equations/signs.
- NOT ABSORBED (read theorem only): no valid direct implication from precisely the inspected parent result.
- UNRESOLVED: final published theorem text or a critical condition inaccessible. Do not treat UNRESOLVED as NOT ABSORBED.

### Parent P1 — Cornes & Hartley (2007), weakest link, published §4 Propositions 4.1–4.2 (Stage30B)

**Parent → child:** common public-good service level \`q=min(x_1,x_2)\` → private effective pair stock \`a=min(a_1,a_2)\` or \`c=min(c_1,c_2)\`; matched contribution profiles with service up to a best-response level → a continuum with \`F_a(c,a)\ge0\`. Identically priced redundant larger private investments cannot be optimal when they do not change the minimum.

**Necessary restrictions/steps:** restrict their contribution technology to a given pair; add two-dimensional stocks and Cournot externalities; create a **third noninvestor**, a political fallback activated by **withdrawal** and a proposer/vote filter \`K>V\`; demonstrate uniform profitable finite deviations under both government continuations and no viable three-private-developer outcomes. None is a consequence of their §4 statement.

**Disposition:** matched-stock continuum, redundant-above-min reductions and weak marginal-best-response condition = **DIRECTLY ABSORBED as component**; joint condition C1 = **PARTIALLY ABSORBED**, not the printed 2007 result. Their generic fallback-free cooperation cannot by itself derive C6 constitutional welfare.

### Parent P2 — Grossklags, Christin & Chuang (2008), §4.2 Result 2 (Stage30B)

**Parent → child:** minimum security effort \`min(x_i)\` → shared compatibility stock \`min(a_i)\`; privately acquired self-insurance outside option → the amount a firm receives if it gives up private development.

**Mapping failure:** insurance is an individual's independent choice, while our withdrawal changes other governments' votes and potentially triggers an **obligatory** public installation by all firms. The amount \`P\` depends on the prescribed legal policy selector. Their outside insurance does not generate the third government's opposite incremental setup cost \`K\` or the constitutional filter \`K>V\`.

**Disposition:** weak link plus fallback and participation threshold **PARTIALLY ABSORBED**; public political fallback correspondence and all SPEs **NOT DIRECTLY ABSORBED**.

### Parent P3 — Caparrós & Finus (2020), JPET Proposition 1 and Appendix A.1

**Parent → child:** an international cooperation agreement with global weakest-link public good \`Q=min_i q_i\` and stability to coalition exit → private bilateral coordination plus the difference between member/nonmember decisions.

**Mapping failure:** global minimum including all agents is not our pairwise minimum among only active compatible firms. Their signatories choose a coalition investment allocation; our firms make independent unilateral investments, and a **different** government subsequently controls the public standard. A membership exit is not the firm's N withdrawal that invokes \`M\`. Missing required national Cournot welfare signs and prescribed agenda.

**Disposition:** public-good coalition instability, bargaining and outside-option ideas **PARTIALLY ABSORBED**; C1–C6 **NOT DIRECTLY ABSORBED**.

### Parent P4 — Harstad (2005), QJE, 2004 WP §5 and Proposition 10

**Parent → child:** political project outcome depends on eligible proposer, majority, and ex-ante investment; an initiator who would lose from the proposal refuses to call a vote, another favorable initiator restores passage → our ex-post rule \`A\cap S(h)\ne\varnothing\` with \`|S(h)|\ge r\`; prior investment changes national policy gains.

**Absorption:** agenda block + profitable sponsor has already been studied. The 3-government set-intersection threshold given a two-government support coalition is a **DIRECTLY ABSORBED general logical component**. No claim of a newly discovered pre-investment/agenda interaction is permissible.

**Mapping failure:** the inspected WP no-transfer Proposition 10 uses a different project/payoff environment and carefully distinguishes investment externalities. It does NOT deliver \`F_a\ge0, F\ge P,K>V\` for a privately established bilateral minimum technology in a Cournot economy, no three-investor exclusion, or \`T\` maximization over all resulting private SPE. The final QJE paper proposition-level version remains **UNRESOLVED**.

**Disposition:** rule/investment/rejection logic = **PARTIALLY or DIRECTLY ABSORBED components**; C1/C5 proof-specific contribution not derived from the inspected WP.

### Parent P5 — Bordignon & Brusco (2006), JPubE; 2003 CESifo WP 996 Propositions 1,5,6,7

**Parent → child:** three-country initial standards selected before a chance of later common harmonization; initial decisions change the subsequent status quo; sub-union and outsider strategic tensions; modified agreement rules can make global harmonization more likely even without compensation.

**Explicit near-collision:** WP **Proposition 5** solves initial noncooperative standard choices anticipating harmonization; **Proposition 6** solves a bilateral subgroup against an outsider; **Proposition 7** finds that permitting a sub-union without unanimous initial consent can make centralization more frequent. These are direct prior examples of endogenous prepolicy standard choices and reform-driven harmonization. A title or Introduction asserting either broadly as first is untenable.

**Mapping failure:** the three initial decision makers are governments choosing national policy positions, not separate producers paying setup \`K\` and commonality expense. There is no explicitly separate home-firm versus home-government preference wedge, privately imposed pairwise-min weakest-link stocks, or full continuous **corporate** pure-SPE correspondence. Their future centralization's standard can be influenced by earlier national choices in their no-commitment variant, whereas ours is a fixed legal public \`O(1,0)\`. Their comparative statistic is not the extremum of a heterogeneous private investment SPE welfare set.

**Disposition:** dynamic three-country standards, future harmonization, partial coalition and constitutional effects = **PARTIALLY ABSORBED**; C1 plus C5 plus C6 **not obtained as direct restrictions** of the examined 2003 WP. 2006 published theorem exactness is **UNRESOLVED**.

### Parent P6 — Takarada, Kawabata, Yanase & Kurata (2020), JPET Proposition 5 / 6

**Parent → child:** 3 symmetric countries and traded goods; regional-versus-multilateral mandatory standard policy; an outsider government loses from integration and can **block an otherwise globally efficient multilateral arrangement**. Publisher-indexed **Proposition 5** proves outsider welfare falls on multilateral harmonization compared to the regional regime when the transboundary parameter \`δ>γ/12\` under Assumptions 1–2. **Proposition 6** reports only a multilateral standard lies in the **core** for \`δ≤γ/12\`, but only a regional standard for \`δ>γ/12\`.

**Direct overlap:** outsider blocking, country coalition switching, the potential gap between world-optimal harmonization and political stability, and fixed-cost standards sensitivity (included in their extensions) are **NOT original general statements** here. This is now among the most important mandatory introductions/references.

**Exact non-absorption distinction:** their countries strategically coordinate **government-set mandatory standards**, and the equilibrium concept is **coalition core** of national standards regimes. Our legal motion is an already fixed, publicly available interface **after** privately developed firm stocks, and the equilibrium is a **sequential pure-SPE** with internal founder firms who weakly prefer private preservation even though their own government strictly prefers public adoption. The source cannot be transformed into a private independent investment \`min\` game simply by renaming a country preference as firm profit: doing so identifies two explicitly conflicting players and breaks the payoff/stage map. Their core does not directly deliver C1's four design/political inequalities nor C2–C6 welfare extrema over **all** first-stage pure SPE.

**Disposition:** world-optimal-versus-politically-blocked harmonization, outsider opposition and regional/multilateral alternatives = **DIRECTLY ABSORBED as broad policy phenomena**; C1/C5/C6 = **PARTIALLY ABSORBED at thematic level; NO direct theorem derivation from the inspected P5/P6**. Full final published proofs only partially accessed via publisher indexed paragraphs.

### Parent P7 — Gandal & Shy (2001), JIE, compared through published abstract & 1996 WP P1–4

**Parent → child:** three governments, regional standard unions, third-country excluded, network compatibility costs and national trade interests. 1996 WP Proposition 2 already admits a privately or nationally beneficial two-member union that excludes the third under particular conversion-cost conditions.

**Mapping failure:** that working-paper government recognition game fixes government decisions at a different stage, uses a different trade/demand structure and does not involve privately chosen triangle \`(c_i,a_i)\` followed by a fixed public mandate with ex-ante sunk setup. **But 2001 published theorem/proof text was not inspected; it would be unacceptable to claim it lacks our result.**

**Disposition:** government recognition/regional exclusion **PARTIALLY ABSORBED**, critical published version **UNRESOLVED** for direct theorem absorption.

### Parent P8 — Klimenko (2009), JIE, published abstract/introduction only

**Necessary transformation:** two-government strategic compatibility instruments → three-country agenda/quorum selector; open private pairwise stock and sunk setup; ex-post public fixed interface and participation; global private pure-SPE set and its maximum. None is demonstrated.

**Disposition:** **UNRESOLVED** for publication-theorem priority; background policy overlap established by published abstract.

### Parent P9 — Farrell & Simcoe (2012), RAND author's 2011–12 paper Propositions 3–7

## 3. A rigorous short reduction of each C1–C7

| Stage30C claim | Strongest short-form reduction attempted | Standalone novelty result |
|---|---|---|
| C1: complete private-SPE iff | Weakest-link matched investments with marginal condition + opt-out participation inequality + an ex-post national political filter \`K>V\` + no triple-investor equilibrium | **Potential substantive novelty only for the integrated game**, not the three elementary constraints in isolation. Parent papers don't supply all those verifiable links. |
| C2: all public SPE welfare invariant | All mandated firms face same final capital cost except initially wasted \`φa_i²/2\`; each can withdraw when \`a_i>0\`; aggregate survivor cost \`3(K+φ/2)\`. | **Accounting+deviation corollary**, not independent general novelty. |
| C3: under broad agenda, initial equilibria exactly all \`N/O(c,0)\` profiles | C1 eliminates L; C2 removes a>0; low-a approval ensures all remaining profiles induce M. | New precise current-game correspondence, but **supporting corollary**, not third major economic discovery. |
| C4: private set expands with fixed K | Founder pays K on either private or public-withdrawal path, so K cancels; outsider newly pays K under M, filter gets weaker when K increases. | Distinct **cost-incidence comparative static**, modest economic content; don't assert actual investment quantity increases. |
| C5: maximum private-SPE welfare attained at canonical pair design | Under \`T_c,T_a>0\`, and \`a\le a_R^*\` for every private SPE, maximize T by reaching greatest legal private coordinates. | **Highly valuable game-specific economic bridge**. | C7: firm favors private but government favors M, outsider rejects | On C1, \`F\ge P\` and \`B>0,K>V\`; consumer-surplus wedge converts negative firm gain to positive national gain. | Common firm/consumer interest wedge **already known**; this case is an effective explanatory mechanism, not a standalone general discovery. |

The strongest viable paper claims **two bundled contributions, not seven novelties**:
A. **Endogenous private investment cannot politically protect a standard where a favorable national agenda remains active; politically protected private standards form an explicitly constrained coordination set.**
B. **The constitution's effect on world welfare can be bounded sharply for ALL privately sustainable and publicly sustainable pure equilibria; eliminating an inefficient equilibrium can also eliminate the private welfare maximum.**

## 4. Comparison failure criteria and priorities

**Kill these novelty claims now:** first multi-country standards coordination, first outsider veto, first preference divergence between producers and consumers/governments, first agenda/investment interaction, first equilibrium multiplicity under minimum technology, first sunk-cost welfare reversal, first endogenous international standardization or trade, first uniform welfare bound as a mathematical comparison.

**Surviving candidate:** current game's C1–C6 joint insight that political protection is supplied by an outsider despite the founders' governments favoring replacement; firms' weakest-link contributions and government-triggered withdrawal lock out other political-support patterns; constitutional reform deletes that private correspondence and admits a **selection-robust** (not expected-welfare) welfare criterion.

**Top priority source-access block:** full published theorem/proof inspection of Gandal–Shy 2001, Klimenko 2009, Bordignon–Brusco 2006, and final complete Takarada 2020 proof/mapping. This is one grouped *published-priority closure* task, not four excuses for unscheduled model expansion.

**Materially narrower economic validity:** only baseline technology + \`η∈[.10,.14],K∈[.54,.56]\`, prescribed sincere voting/proposal selector, pure SPE. Old Stage30 government-fee and unrestricted simultaneous-vote diagnostics invalidate attempts to call this a universal institution result.

The comparison's **NOT DERIVED** judgments are bounded to the versions/theorems actually read. They cannot be upgraded to published literature priority PASS without closing the access ledger. No new full-paper draft is authorized by this Stage6 finding.

## 2. Alternative institutions already tested: negative robustness is INFORMATION, not a bug

### A. Government implementation expenditure g

Stage30 separately pre-registered an alternative where the public choice M bears an additional real resource government cost \(g=.04\) per country, holding corporate compliance costs unchanged. For \(\eta=.14,K=.55\), two founders develop \(O(1,a^*)\) with \(a^*\simeq.5949515\), the outsider N, and ***all three agenda holders eligible*** with \(r=2\). Recomputed policy gains were approximately
\[
(\Delta_1,\Delta_2,\Delta_3)=(-.0233615,-.0233615,-.1964196).
\]
The independent fee diagnostic includes founder and outsider investment deviations and exact root-interval sign checks. L survives in a pure SPE. Therefore **C3 agenda-based elimination and the corresponding C6 single-public-payoff reform comparison do NOT transport automatically** to this altered model. One country's \(g=.04\) equals roughly 2.38% of the baseline gross national public surplus as an illustrative relative measure, not an empirical typical rate.

This failure is **not** proof that an arbitrarily small fee breaks C1–C6: the test shows a particular \(g\) and \(\eta,K\) combination. The fixed-history founder approval condition becomes \(B(c,a)-g\ge0\), giving \(g=B(c,a)\) as a fixed-history vote boundary. This is **not a full equilibrium-cost threshold** because all deviations and costs must be re-solved to classify SPE. Do not market it as the universal reform fee theorem.

### B. Unrestricted simultaneous strategic voting

At \(r=2\), after a legal public proposal, the vote profile (NO,NO,NO) is always a Nash continuation because no single vote can pass a majority. Even at an all-N history where each government strictly prefers M, all-NO is nonpivotal Nash. Using all-NO on every relevant history, the first-stage no-development NNN ending in L is an unrestricted pure SPE. This directly contradicts any extension of C3 to **all** unrestricted political-strategy SPE. The published C1–C6 must therefore say *under the prescribed policy selector*, not *analyzed full unrefined democratic game*. Replacing the rule with a dominance/sincere refinement or sequential votes would require Stage3/4/4A/6 recertification; prose alone cannot fix a changed game.

### C. Coherent technology family / third firm's zero-stock entry

The private-demand rule is discontinuous in the developer count: N→O(0,0) changes a demand branch even when the entrant's real compatibility stock is zero. The earlier Stage30 secondary diagnostic examined but did not certify a differently defined cross-technology \(\eta\) effect on entry. No valid preservation theorem or failure theorem for such an alternative has been established. This is a **HIGH-IMPORTANCE UNTESTED PORTABILITY RISK**, not negative-certificate evidence.

### D. Symmetry and functional forms

No exact result here covers heterogeneous market sizes, nonlinear utilities, negative cross-effects, third-country adoption choices or a network-effect parameter outside the original box. None is needed for accurate internal results. Claiming the original local seven-dimensional neighborhood automatically extends the new C1–C7 is an invalid quantifier transfer. These must be marked not tested, not necessarily false.

## 3. Marginal economic contribution vs the parent's known models

- Cornes–Hartley (2007) and Grossklags et al. (2008): matched minimum investment and participation outside alternatives generate coordination and multiplicity. **Not a new economic law.**
- Harstad (2005): proposer's exclusion, positive winning coalition and policy-influenced investment already known; all-NO issue highlights importance of equilibrium definition.
- Takarada et al. (2020) published journal main text: regional/multilateral standards, outsider rejection and fixed-cost welfare shifts known.
- Bordignon–Brusco (2006) earlier WP: investment/standard choices anticipating future integration known; published details still unavailable.
- The plausible full-paper contribution is **ONLY THE BUNDLED private investment political fallback correspondence + extrema-based all-SPE policy welfare**.

Thus generality is **MODEL/INSTITUTION SPECIFIC**. Two negative diagnostics sharply limit external validity. A restricted pure-theory paper may still be legitimate, but cannot be sold as a general theory of international standardization.

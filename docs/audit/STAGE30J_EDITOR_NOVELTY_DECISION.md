## 3. Strongest publication objection and specific defense

**Referee rejection:**

> The paper adds an agenda-count condition to a weakest-link standardization investment model and then compares the welfare levels of its surviving equilibria. Endogenous compatibility and multiple subgame-perfect equilibria with fixed-cost-dependent welfare reversals are already characterized in Buccella, Fanti, and Gori (2023); government and firms' standardization interests in Barrett and Yang (2001); SSO-induced innovation responses in Nabin, Sgro, and Lahiri (2023); endogenous anticipatory regional policy in Bordignon and Brusco (2006); and a core-stable outsider veto in Takarada et al. (2020). Yet the advertised main phenomenon exists only with an exogenous mandatory public standard, a stipulated sincere policy selector, a specially discontinuous 3-O demand branch and a narrow parameter rectangle. The difference is primarily the number of institutional labels and the numerical fit of inequalities. A nontrivial general economic result or falsifiable institutional prediction is missing.

**Defense licensed by current evidence (no more):**

> The prior results do not map directly onto the full sequential game in which the firms invest independently, separate governments evaluate profit plus domestic consumer surplus, and the noninvesting outsider pays a *new* mandatory setup cost that founders reuse. Conditional on the exact legally prescribed policy selector, this joint structure filters **all** corporate investment profiles, not only a selected oligopoly equilibrium, and allows an ex-ante welfare guarantee against **every** pure equilibrium of the old constitution. But the result is a **narrow benchmark**; no general causal claim about actual legislatures, national welfare Pareto improvement, or other demand families follows.

The defense does not prove uniqueness in scholarly literature; a broader general parent theorem could still absorb the conditional equilibrium classification. This is the relevant independent-notational-difference test.

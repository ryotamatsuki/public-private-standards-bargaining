### Realism kill test
No audited ISO, ITU-T, EU, procurement institution has all of: **(a)** exactly three governments voting by 1/2/3 affirmative votes, **(b)** individual country agenda rights, **(c)** legally binding common fixed technical interface on all three countries' firms, **(d)** no public administrative adoption cost, and **(e)** a rule that votes sincerely even when nonpivotal. These individually plausible components should not be combined into an invented real-world institutional match.

A model-specific *diagnostic theoretical result* can still be a contribution **if** the paper states the thought experiment and a credible microfoundation for the jump between two-/three-developer technological regimes; an implementable reform prescription requires much more institutional detail.

## 3. Diagnosis vs normative benchmark vs implementable policy

**Diagnosed:**
- Founders invest privately but their home governments prefer public, while uninvested foreign government's unused setup K supplies political protection.
- Under exact A/r selector, broad proposer eligibility eliminates private pure SPE.
- Private equilibria exhibit opposite welfare rankings under identical parameters; a reform can improve the infimum while sacrificing the supremum.

**Normative benchmark (limited):**
- On C6 domain, broad proposal access gives world welfare no lower than every old pure equilibrium **iff \(K\le K_W(\eta)\)**.
- Above cutoff, reform's worst-case improvement and best-case loss cannot be combined into unconditional welfare-improving policy. Ex-ante world welfare is not national Pareto improvement.

**NOT implemented / not demonstrated feasible:**
- No institutional mechanism allocates 2 independent government agenda rights to enact a worldwide technical mandate under nationally sincere voting.
- No information mechanism guarantees governments observe every private stock or know true national welfare gains.
- No treaty/country jurisdiction rule enforces international compliance; no IP clearing, bilateral compensation or budget for conversion; no market selection rule, distributional criterion, or endogenous lobbying.
- Thus no claim that ISO should change membership, EU should reassign proposal power or any government should impose a common standard in practice.

For a pure theory manuscript, the absence of a ready-made policy program is **BOUNDED LIMITATION**, not per se fatal. For a policy prescription, it is an **ESSENTIAL FOLLOW-UP** not answered here. Our chosen contribution should be diagnostic and explicit about its legal abstraction.

## 4. Empirical interpretations and cautious testable predictions

These are **conditional structural predictions**, not statistically identified causal evidence:

1. If a previously compatible privately invested group is maintained in equilibrium with firms' continued participation and outsiders uninvested, the strongest motive for political retention may come from the *noninvestor's future installation burden* despite supportive home governments. Testing needs measured network membership, ownership, technical capability, national government policy statements and additional migration costs.
2. Holding other structural primitives and the prescribed rule fixed, a higher K expands the **set of supportable private standards**; it does **not** necessarily raise observed firm-level spending or selected c/a. Observational change is selected equilibria, not directly predictable from set inclusion.
3. Under high enough agenda access and r<=2, all pure-SPE public outcomes have no wasted commonality stock a; this predicts **equilibrium total resource-cost equivalence**, not that nobody ever pays upfront.
4. Welfare gain from a rule reform cannot be inferred from the adoption of a common interface. Under \(K>K_W\), market and technology histories may include both better- and worse-than-public private standards at identical parameter values. Testing requires a credible design/selection model and counterfactual expenditure data.

Falsifiers of *external validity* (not algebra):
- A technological rule that lets an entrant with zero stock join WITHOUT instant change in the incumbents' demand interaction challenges the current O(0,0) branch.
- Licenses/royalties or cost reimbursement eliminate K cancellation/outsider veto.
- A jurisdiction with nonpivotal NO continuations contradicts using the sincere rule as a behavioral equilibrium rather than an imposed institution.

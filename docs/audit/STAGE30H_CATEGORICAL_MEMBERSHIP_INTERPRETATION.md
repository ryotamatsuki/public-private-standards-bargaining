## 1. Proposed half-page economic interpretation: categorical participation versus intensive investment

**Minimal admissible conceptual reading.** The private open-technology choice has **two margins**. The binary decision \`N\` versus \`O(c,a)\` purchases a discrete minimum capability to join a buyer-recognized *technical product family*. Its real, irreversible, one-off installation/testing/integration expenditure is \`K>0\`. Conditional on this binary basic qualification, \`c\` and \`a\` are *incremental* intensive design stocks, not the totality of technological participation. Thus **\`O(0,0)\` is NOT physically equivalent to \`N\`**: it has paid K to achieve the family baseline, although it purchases no additional measured capacity or differentiation stock. \`N\` can still produce its own product under L but lacks the family affiliation, and would have to pay K (plus physical completion expense) to comply after M. An existing \`O\` entrant never pays K again under public adoption. This is consistent with the original resource accounting, without adding a K or changing \`R_i^O=\phi(1-c_i^2)/2\`.

The common incremental technical capacity of *two* recognized members is \`min(c_i,c_j)\`; extra commonality is \`min(a_i,a_j)\`. Consumers experience an enhanced demand intercept \`1+\rho c_{\rm effective}\` only when at least two recognized members create a shared connection; buying basic eligibility alone has no direct additive demand intercept. The distinctive component of \`a\` is **not a pure physical network-effect coefficient**: it has an *endogenous category-exclusivity/market-differentiation return* when **exactly two** registered suppliers form an identifiable bilateral cohort. The outsider \`N\` lacks the baseline family identity, and the common pair design is differentiated from it by \`-\eta a_{\rm pair}\` in their pairwise Cournot cross-effects. If a third supplier pays K and joins the recognized family—even with no incremental \`c_3,a_3\`—the commercial category ceases to consist of exactly two members. It is no longer distinguished *as a category* from that third supplier: the \`-\eta a_{\rm pair}\` **categorical premium disappears immediately**, while the private pair's own \`H_{12}=\gamma_0+\kappa a_{12}-vc_{12}\` and incremental technology-based \`z\` remain. This is why \`H_{13},H_{23}\` jump, but founder demand intercepts do not change.

This interpretation is an **analytical discrete-identity/continuous-quality technology** with network coordination and congestion of category exclusivity. It is **not** a standard purely technical interoperability model where all demand depends continuously only on pairwise equipment compatibility, and it does not claim an empirically calibrated law of standards adoption. Consumers are assumed to observe the product's authenticated family **classification** and the shared incremental technical stock effects encoded by H,z. The demand branch itself is assumed, not derived from consumer beliefs, certification protocol, product engineering or an endogenous club-label equilibrium.

**Crucial wording constraint:** \`a\` affects insiders' own rivalry by \`+\kappa a\` and their contrast with an **outside-family** product by \`-\eta a\`; describing \`a\` solely as "physical interoperable capacity" is misleading. A market identity/brand family may be open to all sellers who pay K, so *no exclusionary IP rights or contractual veto* need be postulated; scarcity and two-member exclusivity result endogenously from the fixed participation fee. When the outsider enters, the previously useful exclusive-cohort identity vanishes in H even if the entrant's incremental technical capability is zero. That is the reduced-form content, not a derived consumer psychology result.

## 3. Actual published/official antecedents and what they do NOT prove

- Besen & Farrell (1994), *Choosing How to Compete: Strategies and Tactics in Standardization*, **Journal of Economic Perspectives** 8(2),117–131, publisher https://pubs.aeaweb.org/doi/10.1257/jep.8.2.117 . Journal text/abstract explicitly distinguish competing *for* a standard from competing *within* a standard, and whether products are compatible with rivals. It supports **why binary competitive categorization matters**; it DOES NOT prove that a zero incremental stock entrant changes Cournot cross-substitution by exactly \`\eta a\`.
- USB Implementers Forum OFFICIAL: https://www.usb.org/getting-vendor-id and https://www.usb.org/compliance and https://www.usb.org/logo-license . A company may obtain a vendor ID by membership or a separate fee; certification/logo for **products** requires actual compliance testing and qualified product listing. **Membership alone is NOT equivalent to technical product conformity.** An \`O(0,0)\` analogue would thus have to acquire *actual minimum certified interoperability funded by K*, not simply become a fee-paying legal member. This supplies an existing institutional **distinction between basic qualifying cost and higher technical features**, not an empirical validation of the special H jump. It also falsifies an overly easy "mere ISO/USB committee membership instantly gives compatibility" explanation.
- ISO official conformity guidance https://www.iso.org/conformity-assessment.html distinguishes standards, independent third-party certification and testing; ISO does not certify products itself. There is NO official reason to attribute product-market classification to ISO committee membership alone.
- Farrell & Saloner (1986), *Installed Base and Compatibility*, **AER** 76,940–955, author page https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/installed-base-compatibility . Installed compatible base may create excess inertia/momentum. That is a different mechanism from the current *category-exclusivity step*.

**Source verdict:** An industry antecedent exists for **binary minimum product qualification funded by a setup cost**, while the *exact all-or-nothing elimination of \`-\eta a\` upon a third baseline entrant* has NO identified empirical or published formal microfoundation. Do not cite any listed source as evidence for that precise functional-form discontinuity, or claim equivalence to USB hardware market.

## 4. One hostile economic review, separate from construction (not blind)

**Adverse question:** If \`c_3=a_3=0\`, how can paying K without additional physical compatibility erase the founders' relative differentiation? Why does the class label improve substitute similarity despite no incremental technical functionality?

**Answer if accepting the narrow interpretation:** \`O(0,0)\` is the *family baseline*, not absence of functionality; K bought a minimum recognized interoperability/certification package. The two founders' \`a>0\` investments deliver bilateral category scarcity against N products. By joining that class, the third firm's classification changes the comparison from a *within-versus-outside* market to *all-within* and removes this specific outsider-positioning channel. No claim that its physical incremental \`c_3\` rises. The entrant's paid K is a real sunk baseline effort, not a transfer to a club governing body or a trademark rent; its use needs to be a real implementation/testing expense consistent with national welfare sums.

**Strongest remaining objection:** A common label does not necessarily erase the *functional* premium of a design implemented by only two firms. Actual certification often requires measurable compliance; merely being a basic compliant product may still leave different advanced functionality and substitution. Here \`-\eta a\` vanishes from **both** founder–entrant H values at once purely on membership count. The assumed *entire* premium removal is an extreme reduced-form restriction; no positive-robustness theorem supports it, and it is used in the C1 global no-three-developer result. A specialist reader may regard this as calibrated market segmentation rather than a portable standardization theorem.

**Reviewer disposition:** **PASS INTERPRETATION — STRICTLY AS A DISCRETE CATEGORICAL PRODUCT-FAMILY BENCHMARK; FAIL AS A PURE PHYSICAL INTEROPERABILITY STORY.** The interpretation is logically/economically coherent without altering the game: K funds the minimum product family qualification, while (c,a) are *above-baseline* incremental stocks, and \`-\eta a\` is *category exclusivity*, not independently usable private software functionality. This is a **bounded interpretive PASS**, not empirical support, derived microfoundation, robust demand result, publication novelty PASS, or unconditional field-editor full-paper GO.

Why not call this a generalized microfoundation? Because a full consumer learning/reputation model would add primitives and its implied H might not equal the stipulated step. One can mathematically write \(U(q|S,c,a)=z^\top q-\frac12q^\top H(S,c,a)q\); positive definiteness makes such conditional consumer choice coherent. But **that restates a status-dependent utility assumption and does not explain the causal jump independently**. Label as a *reduced-form interpretation*, not a derived theorem.

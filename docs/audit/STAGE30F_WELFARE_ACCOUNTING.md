## 1. Reconstruct consumer surplus, national welfare and resource costs from primitives

At each of the three symmetric national markets, inverse demand is represented by a symmetric positive-definite H and intercept z. Per-market quadratic representative-consumer utility is
\[
U(q)=z^\top q-\tfrac12q^\top Hq;
\quad p=z-Hq;
\quad CS=z^\top q-\tfrac12 q^\top Hq-p^\top q
=\tfrac12q^\top Hq .
\]
With Cournot first-order conditions \((H+mI)q=z\), every firm i has per-market equilibrium price \(p_i=mq_i\), so its **three-market** operating profit is \(\pi_i^g=3m q_i^2\). Government i values **its domestic firm's profit in all three markets plus CS in its own single national market**, minus that same firm's real development/compliance costs. Summing exactly over the three governments counts all three national consumer markets once each and all 3 international firm-profit streams once each. No government tax revenues, subsidies, licensing rents, trade tariffs or fiscal transfer are in these national payoff definitions. The setup K and physical stock investment costs are real resource costs, not transfers.

Two identical private founders \((O(c,a),O(c,a),N)\): \(\pi_F^L=3mx^2\), \(\pi_O^L=3my^2\), \(CS_L=C\). Define
\[
F=3mx^2-\phi(c^2+a^2)/2,\qquad
T=2F+3my^2+3C.
\]
Private-path world welfare from date 1 is
\[
\mathcal W_R^{ante}(c,a)=T(c,a)-2K.\tag{1}
\]
The uninvested outsider continues production under L, does NOT pay K. This is important to the political incidence.

After an enacted mandate to pre-existing \(O(1,0)\), the all-three-compliant Cournot allocation has gross firm profit \(\pi_I^g\), domestic CS \(CS_I\). Define \(P=\pi_I^g-\phi/2,\ W_I=\pi_I^g+CS_I-\phi/2\). If public adoption is anticipated from the start and initial private waste is zero,
\[
\mathcal W_M^{ante}=3(W_I-K).\tag{2}
\]
Stage30C C2 proves EVERY public-terminal pure SPE has all initial developers' \(a_i=0\) under the prescribed selector and the stated box, hence (2) is **invariant across public SPE initial vectors**; this is NOT initial-action uniqueness. Individual government welfare likewise equals \(W_I-K\). A single initial open \(O(c_i,0)\) may spend \(\phi c_i^2/2\) at date1 but the public completion expense falls by exactly that amount. It is not costless upfront but identical total costs conditional on M.

If public adoption is **instead imposed after** a private bilateral design \((c,a)\), the two founders have sunk wasted commonality stock and their public final total cost per founder is \(K+\phi(1+a^2)/2\). The outsider's final cost is \(K+\phi/2\). This post-history world welfare is
\[
\mathcal W_M^{post|h}=3(W_I-K)-\phi a^2.\tag{3}
\]
The cost difference between (2) and (3) is historical investment expenditure in TWO founders, not a fresh marginal expenditure at the postdecision date.

## 2. National policy preferences and the preference wedge

Holding fixed the installed private history, the founder **corporate** profit-change from mandate is
\[
\Delta\pi_F=\pi_I^g-3mx^2-\phi(1-c^2)/2
=P-F-\phi a^2/2.
\]
The founder **national government** gain is
\[
B(c,a)=\Delta\pi_F+(CS_I-C)>0
\]
at **every private pure SPE** by Stage30C C1+C7, even though \(\Delta\pi_F\le0\) (weak corporate resistance). Hence \(CS_I-C=B-\Delta\pi_F>0\). The noninvestor government's same-history policy gain is
\[
\Delta_O=V(c,a)-K,\quad V=W_I-3my^2-C,
\]
which is STRICTLY negative at every private pure SPE because \(K>V\) is the political participation condition. The outsider does not act as a founder's ally due to contracting, ownership or corporate lobbying; the political support is generated by **national adoption-cost incidence**.

The social sum of these three exact same-history changes is
\[
\sum_i \Delta_i=2B+V-K
=3W_I-T-K-\phi a^2.\tag{4}
\]
The ex-ante policy comparison, however, is
\[
\mathcal W_M^{ante}-\mathcal W_R^{ante}=3W_I-T-K
=:K_W(c,a)-K .\tag{5}
\]
For the canonical most-welfare private design \((c,a)=(1,a^*(\eta))\), (5) uses \(K_W(\eta)\); the post-history aggregate cutoff is \(K_E(\eta)=K_W(\eta)-\phi a^{*2}\). The difference is a sunk-cost accounting consequence, not a new standalone behavioral mechanism. NEVER substitute (4) for the constitutional ex-ante C6 test.

The founding countries' ex-ante national comparison of a zero-waste public path to the private installed equilibrium is \(B+\phi a^2/2>0\). There is **no time reversal of the same founding government preference**. Call it *firm/home-government preference separation*.

## 3. Why C6 is stronger than old Stage29's two-path accounting

C1 proves the complete set of private pure SPE designs \(\mathcal R(\eta,K)\), rather than assuming the privately optimal \(c=1,a^*\) will be selected. For every old private-admitting constitution this set includes \((1,0)\) and the canonical design \((1,a^*)\), along with a continuum of low-level designs. C5 demonstrates that the latter is the **global world-welfare maximum over the entire privately sustainable pure-SPE set**, not a planner's optimum over all conceivable technologies:
\[
\max_{(c,a)\in\mathcal R}\mathcal W_R^{ante}
=T(1,a^*)-2K.
\]
C2 provides constant world welfare at every public pure SPE. Therefore a reform from an old private-admitting constitution to \(r\le2,\ |\mathcal A|\ge2\) has
\[
\boxed{\forall e_{\rm new}\ \forall e_{\rm old}:
\mathcal W(e_{\rm new})\ge\mathcal W(e_{\rm old})
\iff K\le K_W(\eta):=3W_I-T(1,a^*) .}\tag{6}
\]
This is an *across-constitution*, **ex-ante** and **pure-equilibrium** *universal weak-dominance guarantee*. It is not an average gain, probability statement, national Pareto improvement, realized reform effect from unknown equilibrium selection, or first-best assertion. The final iff after the extrema are established is elementary set comparison; the economic accomplishment is endogenous feasible-set construction plus proof of its welfare-support maximum.

The opposite side of the cutoff is economically material:
\[
K>K_W\Longrightarrow
\inf \mathcal W(E_{\rm new})>\inf\mathcal W(E_{\rm old}),\
\sup \mathcal W(E_{\rm new})<\sup\mathcal W(E_{\rm old}),
\]
where the strict minimum difference is supported by an actual low-welfare private equilibrium (c1,a0) **throughout** the baseline box and the canonical high-welfare private equilibrium above the cutoff. Here \(\mathcal W(E_{\rm new})\) is a singleton welfare value though initial actions are multiple. The new constitution **contracts the distribution of feasible pure-equilibrium welfare to a singleton**, not necessarily toward the optimum.

The precise policy statement is "the reform *guarantees* it will not lower world welfare against any old pure SPE iff (6)," not "the reform always raises welfare" or "reform eliminates inefficiency".

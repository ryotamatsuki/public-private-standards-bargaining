## Frozen definitions and objects (never silently relax)

Fixed parameters:
\[
v=1/100,\ \gamma_0=7/50,\ \kappa=1/50,\ \rho=8/25,\ \phi=7/50,\quad
\eta\in[1/10,7/50],\quad K\in[27/50,14/25].
\]
Three symmetric national markets, three firms, three governments, complete information. At date 1 each firm independently chooses **N** or **O(c_i,a_i)** satisfying \(0\le a_i\le c_i\le1\). **O(0,0) ≠ N**: the former pays K and changes the three-developer demand branch. A binary K-funded categorical family qualification is an *interpretation of the existing primitive*, not a separately verified consumer microfoundation (Stage30H).

An institution is \((\mathcal A,r)\), \(\emptyset\ne\mathcal A\subseteq\{1,2,3\}\), \(r\in\{1,2,3\}\); \(\mathcal A\) is eligible PROPOSERS, not the YES electorate. After observing the investments, the **prescribed sincere government selector including YES-on-tie** adopts the known public \(M=O(1,0)\) iff:
\[
\#\{i:\Delta_i(h)\ge0\}\ge r
\ \land\
\mathcal A\cap\{i:\Delta_i(h)\ge0\}\ne\varnothing.
\]
Firms comply following M; they can continue production after N if L. This is SPE over firms' investment choices **conditional on this fixed policy selector**; unrestricted simultaneous government-vote Nash strategies are EXCLUDED. Mixed equilibria, beliefs, positive government implementation expense, transfers, royalties, asymmetric markets and generalized network technologies are EXCLUDED.

For a bilateral installed private pair \(O(c,a),O(c,a),N\) let \(x,y,C\) be unique Cournot quantities and per-market consumer surplus; \(m=1-v\). Define
\[
F=3mx^2-\frac\phi2(c^2+a^2),\quad
P=\pi_I^g-\frac\phi2,\quad
W_I=\pi_I^g+CS_I-\frac\phi2,
\]
\[
B=\pi_I^g+CS_I-3mx^2-C-\frac\phi2(1-c^2),\
V=W_I-3my^2-C,\quad
T=2F+3my^2+3C.
\]
For \(\eta,K\) in the box:
\[
R(\eta,K)=\{(c,a):0\le a\le c\le1,\ F_a(c,a;\eta)\ge0,\
F(c,a;\eta)\ge P,\ K>V(c,a;\eta)\}.
\]
Let \(a^*(\eta)\in(.30,.61)\) denote the **unique** root of \(F_a(1,a;\eta)=0\). \(K_W(\eta)=3W_I-T(1,a^*(\eta);\eta)\). Note: \(\mathcal R\) uses **strict** veto K>V, weak founder participation F≥P and weak directional \(F_a\ge0\).

## Claim-scope contract: seven mathematically certified baseline results

### C1 — Complete private pure SPE correspondence
**Formal logical scope:** for ALL \((\eta,K)\) in the stated rectangle, for ALL nonempty agendas \(\mathcal A\), ALL quorums r and ALL firms' admissible continuous date-1 actions, a private-terminal pure SPE exists **at a particular private investment profile h** iff, UP TO FIRM LABEL PERMUTATION, h consists of **exactly two** matching developers \(O(c,a),O(c,a)\) and one N, with \((c,a)\in R(\eta,K)\), AND either (i) \(r=3\) with any nonempty \(\mathcal A\), or (ii) \(r\in\{1,2\}\) and \(\mathcal A=\{\text{outsider}\}\).

The iff includes zero/one/triple-development exclusions, every off-path policy/compliance continuation, all asymmetric and unilateral finite c,a deviations; it is NOT "an interior c,a solution" or "any two-proposer threshold alone". An "outsider-only" proposal agenda is defined relative to the profile, not a fixed label-3 theorem (unless coordinates are relabeled).

**Portability: INSTITUTION-SPECIFIC + MODEL-SPECIFIC.** Requires K-funded binary status, pairwise minima, special 3-developer H, Cournot signs, NO second setup K under M and prescribed voting.

### C2 — Public-terminal welfare invariance
For ALL baseline parameters, ALL institutions, ALL **public-terminal pure SPE** e, each initial O investor must have \(a_i=0\) (N firms have no a), and **per firm** public profit \(P-K\), national welfare \(W_I-K\), world welfare \(3(W_I-K)\). The initial action vector is generally NOT unique; the statement is conditional on e already being an SPE ending M. It is NOT an assertion that all possible, nonequilibrium M-terminal histories carry the same sunk cost.

**Portability: MODEL-SPECIFIC** resource accounting and deviation to N under current full game, not general standards welfare invariance.

### C3 — Exact first-stage investment set in a broad-proposer institution
For ALL baseline parameters and ALL \(\mathcal A,r\) satisfying \(r\le2,\ |\mathcal A|\ge2\), the **entire set of initial investment profiles of pure SPE** equals the Cartesian product of choices \(N\) or \(O(c_i,0), 0\le c_i\le1\), for each i; EVERY such SPE ends M. This is not a unique initial profile, and \(O(0,0)\) is not equated with N. Do NOT assert C3 under unanimity. No licensed inference about mixed SPE or unrefined strategic voting.

**Portability: INSTITUTION-SPECIFIC, MODEL-SPECIFIC.**

### C4 — Increasing K expands private SPE *design* support
For every fixed \(\eta\) in interval, every FIXED constitution admitting private L, and **every** \(27/50\le K_1<K_2\le14/25\), \(R(\eta,K_1)\subseteq R(\eta,K_2)\) (weak inclusion). **Existential** strict witness: \(\eta=.14,\ c=.929,\ a=0,\ K_1=.54,\ K_2=.55\). The theorem does NOT say the inclusion is strict for every pair of K or that observed/selected investment intensity increases in K.

**Portability: MODEL-SPECIFIC COST INCIDENCE.** \(K\) cancels from founder withdrawal participation but weakens outsider veto.

### C5 — Maximum private pure-SPE world welfare
For ALL \(\eta,K\) in box and every private-admitting institution,
\[
\max_{(c,a)\in R(\eta,K)} [T(c,a;\eta)-2K] =
T(1,a^*(\eta);\eta)-2K.
\]
The maximizer is **unique as a design pair** \((c,a)\). This does NOT make the full equilibrium profile or government/policy history unique: firms are symmetric and outsider's identity is permutable, and lower-welfare private equilibria coexist. The max is **over pure SPE**, not an unconstrained world planner's feasible technology space. Critical proof: \(T_c,T_a>0\) only in required box and \(a\le a^*\) via \(F_{ac}>0\) on **restricted a≥.30**; global strict \(F_{ac}>0\) is FALSE (already recorded negative diagnostic).

**Portability: MODEL-SPECIFIC.**

### C6 — Constitutions' universal pure-SPE world-welfare guarantee
For ALL baseline \(\eta,K\), ALL old constitutions admitting private SPE and ALL new constitutions with \(r\le2,|\mathcal A|\ge2\),
\[
\left[\forall e_n\in E_{\rm new}^{pure}\ \forall e_o\in E_{\rm old}^{pure}:
W(e_n)\ge W(e_o)\right]
\iff
K\le K_W(\eta).
\]
With K>K_W, every new SPE has a common world-welfare level; the **infimum** of old equilibrium world welfare strictly rises under reform, but its **supremum** strictly falls. Use infimum/supremum, not a closed-form attained minimum unless separately proven. At equality the guarantee is WEAK, not strict. E_old includes BOTH old public and private SPE; ignoring old public payoffs causes quantifier errors. It is an **ex-ante** cross-constitution comparison—not a policy retrofit after sunk histories, expected/socially selected reform payoff, nationwise Pareto, or "first best". The last iff comparing a constant to an endogenous max is algebraically standard; the nontrivial economic dependencies are C1+C2+C5.

**Portability: INSTITUTION-SPECIFIC and MODEL-SPECIFIC.**

### C7 — Political preference separation at every private pure SPE
For ALL baseline parameters/institutions and EACH private pure SPE, founding firms' fixed-history mandate-vs-private profit differences equal \(P-F-\phi a^2/2\le0\) (**WEAK private preference**). Their home-government fixed-history mandate gain is \(B>0\) (**STRICT**), and outsider-government gain \(V-K<0\) (**STRICT**). Founder-country **ex-ante** public-minus-private advantage \(B+\phi a^2/2>0\). No same-government intertemporal "preference reversal" follows. Founder firm indifference is feasible at participation boundary.

**Portability: MODEL/INSTITUTION-SPECIFIC; generic firm-government conflicts are known.**

## Explicit falsifiers of prohibited stronger theorem statements

1. **Unrestricted voting:** at quorum r=2 all-NO is a nonpivotal Nash continuation even if every \(\Delta_i>0\). Hence no unconditional ALL-SPE public mandate result in an unrestricted simultaneous voting game.
2. **Positive public administrative cost:** inherited Stage30 g=.04, \(\eta=.14,K=.55\) sustains L with broad agenda; old C3/C6 are NOT transportable without full alternative-game re-solution.
3. **Unanimity false C3:** stage30C all O(1,0) at r=3 is M; firm0→N leaves L and improves payoff by exact fraction \(15291216325919/134283598086400>0\). So "all N/O(c,0) are public equilibria under ANY r" is false.
4. **Global cross derivative false:** \(F_{ac}(1,0;.10)<0\), so C5 must retain restricted positive \(F_{ac}\) domain.
5. **C4 strictly all pairs false/unproved:** only one specific strict inclusion certified; general monotone inclusion \(\subseteq\), not universal \(\subsetneq\).
6. **C7 strictly firm against M false:** \(F=P,a=0\) boundary gives indifference, while two governments still strictly support M.
7. **Binary family status:** outsider N vs O(0,0) switches H even with zero intensive stocks; smoothing this or treating O(0,0)=N changes game and invalidates derived full C1–C6.

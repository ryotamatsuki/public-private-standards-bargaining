## Editorial identity

**Preferred working title:** *Who Protects Private Standards? Investment, Agenda Rights, and Equilibrium Welfare*

**Research question (one sentence):** When firms invest in a private compatibility family before governments can mandate an already-known public interface, which government protects the resulting private standard and can changing governmental agenda rights improve welfare **regardless of equilibrium selection**?

**Puzzle in one sentence:** Private-standard founders' **national governments** favor replacing their own firms' private standard, yet an **uninvested outsider country** blocks replacement to avoid a new setup cost K.

**Mechanism and two derived results, not two isolated discoveries:**
- **R1 (C1+C3+C7): politically filtered first-stage private equilibrium correspondence.** All *private-terminal pure SPE* are exactly (up to permutation) O(c,a),O(c,a),N, where 0<=a<=c<=1, F_a>=0, F>=P, K>V, and constitutional conditions hold. The founding firms weakly favor the status quo but their national governments strictly support public M, while outsider govt strictly opposes M. With broad agenda |A|>=2 and r<=2, all *pure SPE* terminate in M and all initial profiles have N or O(c_i,0). This is NOT a theorem about unrefined strategic politicians.
- **R2 (C2+C5+C6): full pure-equilibrium welfare support / constitutional dominance.** Every public pure SPE has world welfare 3(W_I-K). Max among *private pure SPE designs* is T(1,a*(eta))-2K and the unique maximizing design is (1,a*). For old **private-admitting** and new **broad** constitutions, each new pure SPE weakly exceeds every old pure SPE iff K<=K_W(eta)=3W_I-T(1,a*). For K>K_W, the new equilibrium-welfare infimum rises strictly, while supremum falls strictly. At eta=.14,K=.55 old private SPE include both below and above public welfare (private-public = -.106147364 and +.048787911).

**Economic insight:** Greater political capacity to enact a uniform interface can **remove both welfare-poor and welfare-superior privately coordinated equilibria**. An institution's effectiveness at imposing a uniform standard and the guarantee that it improves world welfare are distinct. This gives a transparent conditional **equilibrium-selection-robust social-welfare test**, not a causal forecast about actual governments.

### Major comment A1 — What is new beyond 2023/2026 Buccella?

If the government selector is exogenous and does not add a strategic political game, why is it an economically substantive strategic stage rather than mechanically deleting outcomes?

**Required action:** Lead with C1's complete correspondence and C7's 3-government sign pattern; show what counterfactual differences become impossible to compute in a standard firm-only compatibility model. The intuition must come from genuine national profit/CS and K incidence, not an assumed vote pattern.

## Referee B — Public economics / political institutions (NEGATIVE for current broad politics framing; revision possible if narrowed)

### Major comment B1 — This is NOT a full six-player governmental SPE
The proposed model imposes government behavior via a sincere national-welfare proposal/vote selector. In a simultaneous majority r=2 game, all-NO is a Nash continuation because one vote cannot pass the motion. Hence C3's no-private-SPE result is not a theorem about unrestricted strategic governance equilibria. Treating it as one is a fatal mistake.

**Required action:** State government rule as **legally stipulated deterministic decision protocol**, not an equilibrium of politicians; title/contribution must not imply a new general strategic voting foundation. Identify its exact policy relevance and why sincere national behavior is an interesting *counterfactual institution*. If you want to sell a new strategic agenda setting mechanism, a different voting game is necessary and would invalidate reuse of present C1-C6 proof.

### Major comment B2 — Under institutional realism, proposal rights cannot simply expand
In the suggested comparison the old constitution can have r=3 and the new r=2 with |A|>=2. That changes quorum AS WELL AS agenda eligibility; it is invalid to attribute the entire effect to "expanding proposal rights." A cleaner within-game fixed-r=2 comparison is old A={outsider} versus new A={outsider,one founder} for a specified profile. The identity of the outsider is endogenous and depends on investor labels, so a priori A must be defined as a fixed set rather than as a rule that changes after observing which firm invested. Unanimity comparisons must explicitly call out the quorum change.

**Required action:** Two institution counterfactuals separately, with exact A,r and pure equilibrium availability. Do not claim constitutional treatment has a single dimension.

### Major comment B3 — Overly convenient fee/certification assumptions
No government administrative drafting/implementation cost and an already-available mandatory O(1,0) public standard make M artificially cheap, while a private entrant must pay K. Why would an international public authority mandate exactly this c=1,a=0 technology? The g=.04 counterexample shows private L with broad agenda. Intergovernmental IP, transfers and asymmetric implementation are also absent. These may be legitimate simplifying assumptions, but the paper's central policy message "give more proposal rights" is fragile.

**Required action:** Reframe conclusion as a **benchmark identification of welfare support, not an implementable international governance recommendation**. Explicitly acknowledge the negative g and unrestricted-voting diagnostics without using them as "positive robustness". If journal demands political-rule endogeneity, present model is not enough.

### Major comment B4 — Public economic value of worst-case criterion
The universal guarantee [∀old ∀new Wnew≥Wold] is very strong but unusual; does an actual policymaker operate under such ambiguity aversion? If there is no equilibrium selection theory, the criterion may be safe but normative conclusion too broad. Improving the minimum and losing the maximum is mechanically possible when the new welfare set is a singleton located between old extremes. Is that result new, or just the definition of infimum/supremum?

**Required action:** Present C6 as a **selection-robust comparison under complete ignorance of selection**, not an optimal decision rule; emphasize the nontrivial economic work is deriving old equilibrium set and viable welfare upper/lower witnesses. The algebraic comparison itself is neither unexpected nor a new social welfare axiom.

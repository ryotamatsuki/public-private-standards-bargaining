The current paper must not claim to discover outsider veto, efficiency/political stability mismatch, fixed-cost sensitive harmonization, or basic firm/government national-welfare component split.

Why Takarada does NOT imply C1/C5/C6 as a short restriction: Under its country-led policy, each government or national coalition CHOOSES the standard \`s_i\` at the policy stage, and the solution concept is core over government coalition regimes. There is no early-stage separate firm independently choosing \`N\` or two continuous commonality stocks under coordinate-wise pair minima, and no firm withdrawal changing an ex post government proposal outcome. Identifying its countries with our independent profit-maximizing firms contradicts the difference between home-government and home-firm payoff and their opposite directions in every privately sustainable history. Most importantly no proposition maximizes **world welfare over a complete first-stage firm-investment pure-SPE correspondence**, so the C5→C6 welfare statement is not a direct corollary of the displayed parent theorems.

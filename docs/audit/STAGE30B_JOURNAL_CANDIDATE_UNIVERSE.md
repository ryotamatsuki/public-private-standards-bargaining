## Contribution-first discovery contract

This ledger precedes the final ranking in
[the target-direction report](STAGE30B_JOURNAL_TARGET_DIRECTION.md).
It records a bounded field/format screen, not a claim that every journal in the
world was searched. No acceptance probability, impact-factor ordering, or
author-prestige preference is used.

The actual paper candidate is conditional applied theory: three firms invest
independently in continuous compatibility designs before a prescribed
national-welfare policy continuation. The proposed full article connects private
investment stability, the distribution of political support, displacement by
agenda reform, and welfare comparisons across all pure equilibria. The new
Stage30B statements remain construction results awaiting new certification.
The surviving scope is MODEL-SPECIFIC / INSTITUTION-SPECIFIC; the numerical
two-proposer threshold is not a general agenda theorem.

Discovery paths: `IO` = firm compatibility, competition, innovation;
`PUBLIC` = externalities, regulation, collective choice and welfare;
`INSTITUTION` = agenda/veto/governance;
`INTERNATIONAL` = national-welfare spillovers;
`THEORY` = applied games and equilibrium characterization;
`FORMAT` = full article versus note;
`VENUE` = omission check against the closest literature.
The entries below are grouped by readership, **not ranked**.

`YES` means inspected for the provisional fit matrix; `SCREENED` means considered
but excluded from the active ladder with a reason. `NOTE ONLY` changes article
type and is not a full-paper fallback. Exclusion judgments concern this paper,
not a claim that the journal bans all narrow or purely theoretical models.

### J1. Buccella, Fanti & Gori (2023), Journal of Economics 140:141–168
*Strategic product compatibility in network industries*. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00712-023-00834-x
Actual OPEN journal **full main HTML** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00712-023-00834-x, inspected §2,§3, **published Proposition 1**, equations (35)–(38), discussion around Figures 1–3; online appendix not separately inspected. 2023 Pisa WP https://www.ec.unipi.it/documents/Ricerca/papers/2023-290.pdf is a separately identified PREPRINT; publication result sourced from final journal HTML, not WP numbering.

**Published P1 exact economics:** Two firms first decide compatible K vs incompatible NK and choose compatibility level (when endogenous) before Cournot market competition. Under positive network effects, varying a **quasi-fixed compatibility cost Z** drives **unique and multiple SPNE**: K/K only, NK/NK only, or both, with their relative firm-efficiency rankings. In published equation (38) the **social welfare difference** between two compatibility regimes changes sign at a cost/network-dependent \`Z^W(n)\`; P1 and Figures 1–3 map the welfare ranking jointly with equilibrium coexistence. Full compatibility vs incompatibility cannot be described as a newly discovered fixed-cost welfare threshold, equilibrium multiplicity or an original firm-first Cournot compatibility game.

**Reduction attempt:** map published compatibility status K→O and NK→N; Z→our fixed K; network strength n and compatibility k→our (eta,c,a) composition; their third-stage Cournot→ours. At the **component level** the fixed-cost endogenous compatibility decision, equilibrium multiplicity, social-welfare reversals, and comparative statics ARE ALREADY DEVELOPED. **Mapping fails for the complete C1/C5/C6** because there are 2 firms, no third outsider's government or explicit noninvestor setup cost triggered by a later mandatory public O(1,0), no proposal-eligible government set \`A\` or quorum r, no national profit+domestic-CS separated from firm profit and no entire independently invested TWO-DIMENSIONAL continuous minimum-stock equilibrium support. Their equilibrium compatibility levels are corner solutions in the endogenous positive-n case, not our privately sustainable a-continuum of two equal founder stocks with a noninvestor.

**Disposition:** DIRECT absorption of broad "endogenous compatibility, multiple SPNE, fixed-cost welfare threshold" story; C1 fully and C6 constitutional **NOT DIRECTLY DERIVED** from published P1; still potentially a game-specific joint contribution. C5's maximization over a continuum of private SPE is much more specific than the published discrete K/NK welfare ranking. Do not frame C6 as the first welfare cutoff in a compatibility game.
